About Dickson's Garden of Eden

Bringing the freshest fruits directly from Kenyan farms to your table

Our Story

Dickson's Garden of Eden was founded in 2015 by Dickson Mwangi, a passionate fruit farmer from Kiambu County. What started as a small family farm has grown into a trusted name for fresh, high-quality fruits across Nairobi and beyond.

Dickson's vision was simple: to provide families with access to the freshest, most nutritious fruits while supporting local farmers. Today, we work directly with over 50 small-scale farmers across Kenya to bring you the best seasonal fruits.

Our commitment to quality, sustainability, and community has made us the preferred choice for thousands of customers who value fresh, healthy eating.

Our Mission & Values

Freshness

We guarantee the highest level of freshness by sourcing directly from farms and delivering within 24 hours of harvest.

Trust

We build lasting relationships with our customers based on transparency, quality, and exceptional service.

Quality

We maintain strict quality standards, ensuring every fruit meets our high expectations before reaching your table.

Meet Our Founder

Dickson Mwangi

Founder & CEO

Dickson Mwangi grew up on a small farm in Kiambu County, where his family cultivated various fruits and vegetables. His passion for agriculture began at an early age, helping his parents tend to their crops.

After completing his studies in Agricultural Science at the University of Nairobi, Dickson worked with several agricultural cooperatives before starting his own venture. His vision was to create a bridge between local farmers and urban consumers, ensuring farmers get fair prices while consumers enjoy the freshest produce.

Under his leadership, Dickson's Garden of Eden has become a model for sustainable agriculture and ethical business practices. He continues to innovate in farming techniques and distribution methods to deliver the best possible experience to customers.

"Our mission is not just to sell fruits, but to promote healthy living and support our local farming communities. Every fruit we deliver carries the story of a farmer's dedication and the richness of Kenyan soil."

Dickson Mwangi
